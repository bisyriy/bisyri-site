Common questions

Everything you need to know before reaching out.

How much does a website cost? +
A starter landing page starts from $250. A business site (up to 5 pages) from $650. An e-commerce store from $1,300. These are starting points — final price depends on scope. A single new client from a well-built landing page typically covers the investment within the first month. I'll give you a clear quote after a short conversation.
How long does a website take to build? +
A landing page is typically 5–7 days. A full business site takes 2–3 weeks. An e-commerce store is 3–4 weeks. Timeline depends on how quickly you can review and provide feedback.
Do you work with international clients? +
Yes — all work is done remotely. I've built and grown websites for businesses in Iceland, the US, China, Southeast Asia, and beyond. Timezone is never a barrier.
What is AEO and why does it matter? +
AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) is the practice of making your website the source that ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity cite when someone asks a question in your industry. As more people search by asking AI instead of typing keywords, being cited in those answers is a direct pipeline to new customers — traffic that traditional SEO alone can't reach.
Can you handle SEO as well as the website build? +
Yes. I've grown new, zero-authority domains to Domain Rating 21–25 — building 1,280+ referring domains across the portfolio. Businesses that combine a solid website with SEO from day one typically see organic leads within 90 days, leads that keep arriving without ongoing ad spend. I can build your site and grow it, or focus only on SEO for an existing site.
What platforms do you build on? +
WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, and custom-coded systems — the platform is matched to the project, not the other way around. For custom systems (like the Hotel Vos guest check-in app, a restaurant POS, and a property management system I built from scratch) I work directly with APIs and custom code.
How do we get started? +
Send me a message via the contact form or WhatsApp with a brief on what you need. I'll reply within 24 hours with a few questions, then send a clear proposal with pricing and timeline.
Do you work with small businesses? +
Yes — all sizes. The strongest ROI I've seen consistently comes from small business projects. A well-built, SEO-optimised service page can generate consistent inbound leads for years — often paying for itself many times over within the first 12 months. Every project gets the same attention regardless of budget.
