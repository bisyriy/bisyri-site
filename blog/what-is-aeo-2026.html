What Is AEO and Why It Matters in 2026

Bisyri·3 Aug 2026·6 min read

For years, ranking on Google was the goal. Get on page one, capture the click, bring the visitor to your site. That was SEO — and it still is.

But something changed. More people now get their answers directly from AI — from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's AI Overviews, and Bing Copilot — without ever clicking a traditional search result. They ask a question and get a cited, synthesised answer.

That's where AEO comes in.

What Is AEO?

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimization. It's the practice of structuring and writing your content so that AI systems can extract, understand, and cite it — turning your website into the source an AI quotes when someone asks a relevant question.

Unlike traditional SEO, which aims to rank pages in a list, AEO aims to become the answer itself.

Simple definition: SEO gets you on the list. AEO makes you the answer before there is a list.

SEO vs. AEO — What's the Difference?

Traditional SEO optimises for crawlers, links, and authority signals to win a position in Google's blue-link results. It's a visibility game — get to page one, earn the click.

AEO optimises for AI extractability. That means clear structure, explicit answers, well-formed schema markup, and authoritative factual content that an AI can quote with confidence.

The core distinction:

  • SEO competes for a position on a ranked list
  • AEO competes to be the cited answer — often the only one shown

In 2026, these are not competing strategies. You need both. But most businesses are still 100% focused on SEO and haven't started thinking about AEO.

Which AI Engines Should You Optimise For?

The main answer engines right now:

  • Google AI Overviews — appears at the top of Google results for many queries, summarising and citing pages
  • ChatGPT Search — OpenAI's browsing-enabled model, used by hundreds of millions of people worldwide
  • Perplexity — a dedicated AI answer engine that cites sources heavily and is growing fast
  • Bing Copilot — Microsoft's AI-first search, integrated into Windows and Edge
  • Claude — Anthropic's assistant, increasingly used for research queries

Each pulls content differently, but they share common patterns: they prefer clean, well-structured content with explicit answers, proper schema markup, strong entity clarity, and high-trust signals.

How Does AEO Actually Work?

Here are the five levers that make a page more likely to be cited by AI:

1. Answer questions directly

Put the direct answer in the first sentence or paragraph of each section. AI systems extract the clearest, most direct answer — don't bury it in context or qualify it to death.

2. Use structured data (schema markup)

FAQ schema, How-To schema, Article schema, and LocalBusiness schema all help AI systems parse your content and understand what it represents. This is the technical backbone of AEO — and most sites are missing it.

3. Create entity clarity

Be explicit about who you are, what you do, and who you serve. "Bisyri is a freelance web designer and SEO specialist working remotely with travel, hospitality, and e-commerce businesses worldwide" is more AEO-friendly than vague brand language.

4. Earn citations and authority signals

AI systems learn what sources are trustworthy partly from links and mentions across the web. Domain authority built through traditional SEO directly supports AEO performance. They feed each other.

5. Format for extractability

Use clear headings, short paragraphs, bullet lists for enumerable things, and numbered steps for processes. AI models extract structured content more reliably than long, unbroken prose.

Is AEO Right for Your Business?

If your customers are searching for answers — not just navigating to a specific site — AEO is relevant to you. That covers almost every service business, professional, and information-based brand.

It's especially powerful in niches where AI search is already highly used: technology, finance, health, travel, marketing, and professional services.

Where to Start

The lowest-friction first step is a content and schema audit. Look at your top ten pages and ask: could an AI extract a clear, citable answer from this page? Most pages can't — and that gap is the opportunity.

Specifically, check for:

  • Direct answers near the top of each section (not buried after three paragraphs of setup)
  • Missing or incomplete schema markup — a free Screaming Frog crawl will show you what's there
  • Vague entity language — if your About page doesn't clearly state who you are and what you do, an AI won't either
  • FAQ sections that could be marked up with FAQ schema

That audit takes a few hours and surfaces the highest-impact changes to make first.

Once you know what's missing, the implementation is straightforward. Read How to Get Cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity & Google AI Overviews for the step-by-step process — schema markup, content restructuring, entity clarity, and how to verify it's working.
