Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Applies to bisyri.co

This page explains what cookies bisyri.co uses, why, and how you can control them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help sites remember preferences, maintain sessions, and understand how visitors use the site.

What cookies does bisyri.co use?

bisyri.co itself does not set any first-party tracking or advertising cookies. The site is static HTML — no login sessions, no personalisation, no tracking pixels set by Bisyri.

However, third-party services embedded in or linked from the site may set their own cookies:

ServiceWhen setPurposePolicy
Stripe When you click a payment link or visit the checkout page Fraud prevention, checkout session management stripe.com/cookie-settings
Google Fonts On every page load Font delivery — Google may log the request. No tracking cookie is set by the font embed itself. policies.google.com/privacy
Kit (ConvertKit) If you interact with an embedded form Form submission tracking, subscriber deduplication kit.com/privacy

How to control cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • See what cookies are stored and delete them individually
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block all cookies (note: this may break some site functionality)

Instructions for common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Changes to this policy

If we add new third-party services that set cookies, we will update this page. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
