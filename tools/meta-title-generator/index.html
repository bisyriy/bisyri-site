Character count guide

Title tags

50–60 characters is the sweet spot. Google truncates titles over ~60 characters in results. Under 40 characters leaves ranking opportunity on the table.

Meta descriptions

140–160 characters is optimal. Google truncates at ~160 characters on desktop and ~120 on mobile. Longer descriptions may still be used — just cut off.
