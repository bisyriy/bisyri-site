Common questions

What's the difference between Starter and Growth?

Starter gets you a professional website that looks great and loads fast. Growth adds the local SEO layer — Google Business Profile setup, local schema markup, directory citations, and targeting so your business shows up when someone in your area searches for what you do. For most local businesses, Growth pays for itself in one or two extra jobs.

How long does local SEO take to work?

Local SEO isn't instant — Google needs time to recrawl and rerank. Most clients start seeing movement within 30–60 days and meaningful ranking improvements within 90 days. The 30-day ranking check included in Growth confirms the trajectory. Done For You clients get monthly updates to keep compounding those gains.

What does the AI lead follow-up actually do?

When a potential customer fills out your contact form, the AI sends them a professional reply within minutes — while you're on a job, asleep, or otherwise unavailable. It confirms their enquiry, sets expectations on response time, and keeps them warm. Speed to reply is one of the biggest factors in whether a lead converts. Most trades businesses take hours or days to respond; you'll reply in minutes.

I already have a website. Can I just get SEO or AI automation?

Yes. If your existing site is well-built, we can skip the rebuild and go straight to local SEO setup or AI automation. Get in touch and describe your setup — I'll let you know if it's a good foundation or if a fresh build would give better results faster.

Can I cancel Done For You?

Yes, anytime after the 3-month minimum. The 3 months are needed because the website build, SEO setup, and AI configuration all happen in that window — it takes that long to deliver the full package. After month 3 it's month-to-month and you can cancel with 30 days' notice. You keep everything that's been built — it's yours.

Do I need to prepare anything?

Just a logo (or even just your business name), a couple of photos of your work, and rough notes about what you do. The questionnaire walks through everything — it takes about 15 minutes. If you don't have photos, I'll use professional stock images that match your trade. No writing skills needed; I handle the copy.

How is this different from Wix or hiring a local agency?

Wix and Squarespace charge $20–$40/month every month and look like DIY. A local agency charges $3,000–$8,000 for the same result, takes 6–8 weeks, and bills hourly for every tweak. My packages are fixed scope, fixed price, delivered in days not months, and you own everything outright — no platform lock-in, no hourly meter running.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

For Starter and Growth: if after the revision round you're still not satisfied, I'll refund you in full. No arguments. For Done For You: if the work in month 1 doesn't meet expectations, we'll either fix it or part ways with a partial refund. In practice the questionnaire and video review process catches everything before launch.
